Design a small educational quadcopter drone concept for engineering planning and simulation.

The system should include:
- ROS2 architecture plan
- KiCad electronics planning artifact
- mass budget
- thrust-to-weight calculation
- motor and propeller assumptions
- battery and estimated flight time calculation
- payload margin
- center-of-mass assessment
- material recommendation
- CAD concept script
- validation report
- provenance record

The design must clearly separate verified calculations, assumptions, missing information, and safety limitations.

This is not a flight-certified drone design. It is an educational engineering benchmark.
