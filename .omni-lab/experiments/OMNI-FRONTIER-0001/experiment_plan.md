# OMNI-FRONTIER-0001 — Experiment plan

Codex's broader counter-hypothesis is better supported than my original narrower one, and I can now partially confirm one of its two predicted cases by static trace rather than by trusting Codex's stated output: with `CONCLUSION_STATES = (CONFIRMED, REFUTED, SUPPORTED, PROMISING_UNPROVEN, INCONCLUSIVE, BLOCKED_BY_REQUIRED_EVIDENCE)` and `_CONCLUSION_STATE_TOKEN_PATTERN` built as a single `\b(...)\b` alternation searched with `re.search` (leftmost-position match, not leftmost-alternative), the string `\"Not CONFIRMED; actually REFUTED.\"` deterministically extracts `CONFIRMED` — because `CONFIRMED` occurs at an earlier character offset than `REFUTED` in that string, independent of tuple order. This confirms the mechanism is a purely positional prose-parsing artifact, reproducible with zero cross-agent disagreement, which is exactly Codex's claim that the flaw is broader than a missing Codex-veto path. The next step is to run Codex's two proposed cases end-to-end through the real orchestrator/adapter machinery (not just the isolated regex) to confirm neither `ensure_multi_agent_participation`, `SelfCertificationError`, nor any other guard intervenes before archival in either case.
